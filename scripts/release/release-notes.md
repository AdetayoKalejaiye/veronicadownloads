Veronica 0.1.0 for Windows and macOS.

| Your computer | Download |
| --- | --- |
| Windows (64-bit) | `Veronica-windows-x64.exe` |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) | `Veronica-macos-arm64.zip` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Veronica-macos-x64.zip` |

These builds aren't signed by a paid certificate yet:
- **Windows** may show "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** unzip, drag Veronica to Applications, and open it. If macOS says it can't be checked, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

Each installer passed Veronica's packaged runtime checks before upload; `downloads.json` lists their sizes and SHA-256 hashes. `Veronica-library-sources.zip` holds the unmodified sources of the open-source libraries the app ships.
